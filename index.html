A plan that follows you

From what you actually do

Your plan starts from your own runs, read from Apple Health or Garmin Connect or entered by hand, and grows with you from your first walk-run to the marathon.

On your wrist

Each run goes to your Apple Watch or Garmin as a structured workout, with intervals, recoveries and pace targets for the next seven days. When your plan changes, your watch follows.

Adjusted every day

A short morning check-in and last night's sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate, compared with your own normal range. When you are run down or something hurts, the hard run turns into an easy one.

See every change first

Before your plan changes, you see which days change, what they become and why. Every version of your plan is kept, together with what caused it.

Built for real life

Life happens

Move a session to another day and the plan checks that your week still works. After an illness, an injury or a break, you get a staged way back, with its own path for the Achilles, calf, shin, hamstring and knee.

Strength that fits your day

In the gym, at home, or as a 10-minute strength snack, with sets, reps and rest times. Balance work from 65. Or none at all, if you only want to run.

Every stage of life

Pregnancy, the return after birth, menopause, pelvic floor and bone health: LifePace asks, and your plan changes where it should.

Race day, if you want one

An optional finish-time estimate from 5K to the marathon, a taper with a short sharpener before the race, carbohydrate targets for long runs, and easier targets when it is hot.

See where you stand

Your fitness age

Your VO2max, estimated from your own runs, becomes a fitness age based on the HUNT study of healthy adults in Norway. A habits score out of 100 shows how your weeks add up for long-term health.

How it went

Route, pace and heart rate, time in each zone, and your laps against the planned pace. A score shows how closely you followed the plan, not how fast you were.

Rules you can trust

Your plan follows written training rules, each traced to sports science or marked as our own choice, and tells you why it changed. No AI decides your training.

Your data, protected

Stored encrypted on servers in the EU, health data with a key of its own. Each health topic only with your consent, and you can delete everything at any time.

Your road to a healthy baseline

Milestones from walker to walk-runner to runner, with about how many weeks it takes to reach the next one.
