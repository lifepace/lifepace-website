Run for the rest of your life.

A running plan built for long-term health, with room for your next race.

From what you actually do

Your plan starts from your own runs, read from Apple Health or entered by hand, and grows with you from your first walk-run to the marathon.

Adjusted every day

A short morning check-in shapes today's session: when you are run down or something hurts, the hard run turns into an easy one.

Careful progression

Gradual increases, regular recovery weeks, optional strength work, and health questions that recommend a professional when a symptom needs checking.

Every stage of life

Pregnancy, the return after birth, menopause, pelvic floor and bone health: LifePace asks, and your plan changes where it should.

Rules you can trust

Your plan follows written training rules, each traced to sports science or marked as our own choice, and tells you why it changed. No AI decides your training.

Your data, protected

Stored encrypted on servers in the EU, health data with a key of its own. Each health topic only with your consent, and you can delete everything at any time.
