2. What LifePace is

LifePace makes a running plan from what you enter and from the runs and health values you allow it to read, and adjusts it every day from your morning check-in. The plan follows written training rules; no artificial intelligence decides your training.

LifePace gives training guidance, not medical advice. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and does not replace a doctor, physiotherapist or midwife. Where a health answer needs checking, LifePace recommends that you see a professional.

LifePace is for adults only (18 and over).

3. Your health comes first

  • Ask a doctor before you start if you have a known heart, metabolic or kidney disease, or symptoms that may point to one, or if you are unsure whether running is safe for you. If you are pregnant or have recently given birth, talk to your midwife or doctor about your running.
  • If you feel chest pain, unusual breathlessness, dizziness or faintness, or joint pain while running, stop and get medical advice before you run again. Do not wait for the plan to change.
  • You decide whether, where and how you run. Follow traffic rules and adapt to weather, terrain and how you feel on the day.
  • The plan can only be as good as what you tell it. Answer the health questions and the check-in truthfully, and update them when something changes.
