Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data LifePace processes, why, and what rights you have. It covers the LifePace app for iPhone, the LifePace service behind it, and this website. LifePace is in development and not yet available. This policy describes how it will work at launch, and we will update it before then if anything changes.

2. In short

  • You use LifePace with an account. Your plan is made on our servers in the European Union, from what you enter and what you allow the app to read.
  • Health data is used only with your explicit consent, given separately for each topic. Withdrawing a consent deletes that data.
  • Your data is stored encrypted, and health data is additionally encrypted with a key of its own for each user.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking in the app or on this website. Usage statistics are sent only if you switch them on. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time, and your data is deleted with it.

3. Your account

You sign in with an email address and a passkey or password, or with Sign in with Apple or Google. Sign-in, including passwords and two-factor authentication, is run for us by a sign-in provider (section 9). We store only the identifier the sign-in provider gives your account, never your password. If you choose Apple or Google, that company confirms who you are under its own privacy terms.

Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. What LifePace processes

Training data

Your running level and experience, the runs you log or allow the app to read, the days and time you have for training, your preferences (for example whether you want strength work), your goal, and the date and place of a race you enter. We use it only to make, adapt and explain your own training plan. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Health data

Your answers to the health questions (for example known heart, metabolic or kidney disease, symptoms, a doctor's clearance, medication, injuries and pain, eating habits, periods, pregnancy or a recent birth, menopause, pelvic floor and bone health), your height and weight, and your daily check-in (how you slept and feel, and any pain). We use it only to make your plan fit your health, for example to hold back hard sessions or to recommend that you see a professional. Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), section 5.

You can withdraw Apple Health access at any time under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → LifePace.

If you connect a Garmin account, Garmin sends us your activities (date, distance, duration, pace, heart rate) and, only with your consent for daily readiness, your sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV) status. Disconnecting Garmin deletes the data received from it. The connection is not available yet.

5. Consent for health data

Health data is grouped into topics, and each topic is used only after you give your explicit consent to it in the app. The topics include:

  • women's health: pregnancy, return after birth, periods, contraception, menopause;
  • pelvic floor and bone health;
  • heart and medication, including blood pressure;
  • energy and eating;
  • daily readiness: sleep, HRV, how you feel, pain, cycle symptoms.

Each topic says in the app what it changes in your plan. Without your consent, the data of a topic is neither stored nor used. We record which consent you gave and when, to be able to show it. You can withdraw a consent at any time in the app, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR): the data of that topic is deleted, including data not yet sent, and your plan stops using it and tells you so.

6. Where and how it is protected

  • Your data is stored on servers in the European Union and travels encrypted.
  • It is stored encrypted. Health data is additionally encrypted with a key of its own for each user, so a copy of the database alone reveals none of it.
  • We access your health data only with a recorded reason, for example when you ask us for help, and every access is logged.
  • Our server logs record the time, the address called and the result of each request, never what you enter.

7. How long it is kept

  • As long as you use LifePace. Your data is deleted 24 months after your last sign-in, and we email you 30 days before. It is deleted at once when you delete your account. Backups are deleted within their rotation (35 days).
  • Data as received from Apple Health or Garmin is kept for 90 days. After that only what your plan needs is kept, for example a run's date, distance and duration.
  • Each version of your plan is kept with the data it was made from, so every change to your plan can be explained.

8. Usage statistics

Only if you switch on "Help improve LifePace" in the app; it is off until you do. Then, after your plan is shown, the app sends a report without any account or device identifier: the kind of plan (goal, phase, number of runs and hard sessions), and your sex, age, height, weight, weekly distance, longest run and runs per week. Our server turns these into ranges (for example age 40 to 49) and counts them per week. The exact values are not stored, two reports cannot be linked to each other or to you, counts too small to hide a person are not kept, and plan decisions about your health are never counted. The counts are deleted after 24 months. We use them to learn which plans LifePace makes and for whom. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, for height and weight, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you withdraw by switching the setting off.

9. Who else receives data

  • Our hosting provider runs our servers in the European Union, as a processor bound by a data processing agreement.
  • Our sign-in provider runs your account's sign-in in the European Union, as a processor bound by a data processing agreement.
  • A weather service: if you enter a race location, our server asks for the forecast there, sending only the location rounded to about 11 km.
  • Apple, Google and Garmin only as far as you sign in with them or connect them.

We will name the hosting provider, the sign-in provider and the weather service, with their locations, here before LifePace launches. Nobody else receives your data.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). In the app you can export your data, withdraw each consent and delete your account.

To exercise a right, write to the address in section 1. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work.

12. Who LifePace is for

LifePace makes plans for adults only (18 and over) and is not directed at children. It gives training guidance, not medical advice. If in doubt, ask a doctor before you start.

We will update this policy before LifePace launches and whenever the app or our processing changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
